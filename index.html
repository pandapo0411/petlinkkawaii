Overview

Pet Link Kawaii is a casual tile-matching game developed by Hùng Lê. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information stored by the app

The app stores game progress locally on your device, including your unlocked levels, stars, total score, in-game item inventory, and pet collection. This information is used only to keep your progress and is not sent by the app to the developer.

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

Advertising

The app displays ads and may offer rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process device and online identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Please review Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for more details. You may manage ad personalization in your Google account or through your device settings.

Permissions and network access

The app uses internet and network-state access to load and show ads. These permissions are not used by the developer to collect gameplay information from your device.

Children

Pet Link Kawaii is intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising is served by Google and is subject to Google’s applicable policies and settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your local game progress remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. To remove it, use your device’s app settings to clear storage or uninstall Pet Link Kawaii.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer: Hùng Lê at panda.luden14@gmail.com.

By using Pet Link Kawaii, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
